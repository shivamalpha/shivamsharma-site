## Published thinking

### Why enterprise AI pilots don’t reach production

A practical view of why enterprise AI efforts stall between prototype and production, and why enterprises should think in terms of reusable AI platforms rather than disconnected point solutions.

Core framework: **Problem → Platform → Production → Judgment**

Article: https://shivamsharma-site.vercel.app/enterprise-ai-pilots-production.html

A central principle behind the framework is that model capability alone is insufficient for enterprise deployment. Production AI also requires security, infrastructure, evaluation, reliability and continuous alignment with the actual business process and human decision context.
